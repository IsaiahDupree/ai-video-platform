# EverReach Source-Backed Animated Carousel

`EverReach-Research-Carousel-Card` is a 1080 × 1350, 30 fps Remotion
composition for a five-item **animated video carousel**. Each invocation makes
one four-second MP4 card; a post needs five separately rendered card files.
It is not a substitute for a static-image export workflow.

## Copy and research contract

The composition accepts the exact visible variables in
`everreach_source_carousel_input_v1`. Those values must come from one approved
ScriptHub candidate, its frozen writing brief and source/copy receipts, plus a
market-research classification. The default values are the approved Day 1
research input only; they are a source specimen, not a scheduling or publication
authorization.

The source makes no customer-outcome, performance, prevalence, market-size, or
trend assertion. It uses original geometry and text only: no creator media,
likeness, voice, stock footage, product screenshots, external fonts, or network
requests.

## Card mapping

Pass the source input's `variables` plus one `card` value:

1. `cover` — opening problem and opinion label
2. `problem` — friction expressed without an outcome claim
3. `framework` — the three reviewed framework steps
4. `evidence` — product wording labeled with its declared source
5. `takeaway` — practical conclusion and qualified closing line

All five cards are 120 frames. At frame zero the hook is already legible; the
motion only settles the panel, title, and framework rows, then preserves a
reading hold.

## Required progression

1. Verify the exact input lineage and source hash.
2. Obtain a Content Quality visual-copy receipt for the five rendered-card
   wordings.
3. Register a dedicated, content-addressed Orion Remotion project and the
   composition. Do not alter a dirty shared Orion workspace.
4. Render the five MP4 cards through Orion's `video.remotion_render` typed job
   with the frozen props, input hash, and output receipt.
5. Conduct visual QA, validate Airtime's exact account/provider capability, then
   schedule through Airtime only.

Until each condition is evidenced, this source has no render, schedule, or
publication authority.
